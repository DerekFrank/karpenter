# Support Policy

The Karpenter version support policy covers both `kubernetes-sigs/karpenter` and [`aws/karpenter-provider-aws`](https://github.com/aws/karpenter-provider-aws), which are released together at the same version.

Every 6 months, a minor release is designated as a Long-Term Support (LTS) release and supported for 12 months. Regular (non-LTS) releases are supported only until the next minor release is published.

See [aws/karpenter-provider-aws SUPPORT.md](https://github.com/aws/karpenter-provider-aws/blob/main/SUPPORT.md) for the full policy, the current LTS releases and their EOL dates, and which fixes are eligible for backport.
